# **Some of my favorite video games!**
## I'll provide their links below:

### 1. [Genshin Impact](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/)
My favorite **Open-World RPG** with a beautiful world that I've been playing for years! :blush:

![Genshin](https://cdn.wccftech.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/WCCFgenshinimpact2.jpg)

### 2. [Omori](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1150690/OMORI/)
A very emotional **Psychological Horror** where the main character Sunny struggles with a grave mistake he made a few years ago. :disappointed_relieved:

![Omori](https://media.wired.com/photos/5ffc9bd51de419cb67c9fb03/3:2/w_2560%2Cc_limit/_omorisomething.jpg)

### 3. [Doki Doki Literature Club](https://store.steampowered.com/app/698780/Doki_Doki_Literature_Club/)
Another **Psychological Horror** that has a deceivingly cute appearance and breaks the fourth wall to target you, the player. :fearful:

### 4. [Valorant](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/)
A **First-Person Tactical Shooter** where you play different agents to fight 5v5 on randomized maps.
(This one has made me rage a few times. :sob:)

### 5. [Cry of Fear](https://store.steampowered.com/app/223710/Cry_of_Fear/)
A **Psychological Horror** that is much more intense when it comes to the horror factor, where you face nightmarish creatures and delusions. :cold_sweat:
